EAA checklist for online shops: 12 barriers to fix first

6-minute read · Updated November 2026

Most online shops fail on the same few points. Whether you use Shopify, WooCommerce or a custom platform, start with these twelve. Each one is part of WCAG 2.2 Level AA, the reference used for the European Accessibility Act.

  1. Low contrast. Light grey text on white fails. Body text needs at least 4.5:1.
  2. No visible focus. Keyboard users must see where they are. Never remove the focus outline without a replacement.
  3. Hidden focus. Sticky headers, cookie banners and chat bubbles must not cover the focused element.
  4. Missing alt texts. Every product image needs a short, specific description.
  5. Unlabelled variant selectors. Size and colour swatches must say what they are and which one is selected.
  6. Colour-only messages. “Out of stock” shown only in grey or red is invisible to many customers.
  7. Silent cart. When a product is added, the confirmation must be announced to screen readers.
  8. Unnamed icon buttons. Plus, minus, trash and heart icons need accessible names.
  9. Form fields without labels. Placeholders are not labels.
  10. Vague errors. “Invalid input” is not enough; say what is wrong and how to fix it.
  11. Keyboard traps in payment widgets. Test the full checkout with Tab, Enter and Space.
  12. Small touch targets. Buttons should be at least 24 × 24 pixels.

Automated tools find about a third of these problems. The rest needs a manual check with the keyboard and a screen reader, which is why a structured checklist matters.
